Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects when you use the personal expense tracking application (the "Service"), how that information is used, and the choices you have around it.

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect the information you provide, such as your name, email address, and password. When you use the Service, we store the expense records you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions — along with basic account activity like sign-in timestamps.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to operate the Service: to authenticate your account, save and display your expenses, and generate summaries and breakdowns of your spending. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in the Service's database and retained for as long as your account remains active. You can edit or delete individual expenses at any time, and you may request deletion of your account and associated data.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your expense data with third-party services. Where the Service relies on external infrastructure — such as hosting or font delivery — those providers may process limited technical data (like IP address or browser type) solely to deliver their service, and do not have access to your financial records.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact details provided on your account or with your Spendly administrator.
